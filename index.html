2. Easy-Dissolve Comparison

We provide footage comparing the dissolvability of Daily Fiber Mix and Metamucil. You can include this footage in your video when it fits your concept. If you prefer to recreate the comparison yourself, follow the steps below:

  • Prepare the water — Fill two clear glasses with approximately 8 oz of drinking water each. Do not use too little water. Water at around 60°C / 140°F is recommended. Do not use ice water or add ice.
  • Open the packets — Cut open one corner of each packet with scissors. Do not cut straight across the entire top.
  • Add the products gradually — Pour each powder slowly and continuously into its glass instead of emptying the entire packet at once.
  • Mix and let stand — Gently swirl each glass, stir with a spoon, or use a shaker cup. Let both mixtures stand for approximately 30 seconds.
  • Show the final result — Film both glasses side by side once the water becomes clear, showing the final appearance and texture.
